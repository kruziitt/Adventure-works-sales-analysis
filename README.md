# AdventureWorks Sales Performance & Business Insights

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing the AdventureWorks business dataset to evaluate sales performance, customer behaviour, product performance and regional sales contribution.

The objective is to transform raw business data into meaningful insights that can support business decision-making, identify performance trends and highlight opportunities for improvement.

## Business Objectives

- Evaluate overall sales and revenue performance.
- Identify top-performing products and product categories.
- Analyse customer purchasing behaviour.
- Assess sales performance across different territories.
- Identify trends and patterns that could inform business decisions.

## Tools & Technologies

- SQL (DuckDB) – Data extraction, querying and analysis.
- Python (Pandas) – Data cleaning, exploration and analysis.
- Microsoft Excel – Data inspection and validation.
- Power BI – Data modelling, visualisation and reporting.

## Project Workflow

1. Data exploration and understanding.
2. Data cleaning and preparation.
3. SQL queries and business analysis.
4. Exploratory data analysis using Python.
5. Data visualisation and dashboard development.
6. Interpretation of findings and business recommendations.

## Key Findings

1. Strong overall sales: AdventureWorks generated approximately $109.85M in revenue across around 31K orders and 275K
units. Average order value was approximately $3.49K.
2. 2013 was the strongest year: Revenue increased from 2011 through 2013, reaching roughly $44M in 2013, before falling to
roughly $23M in 2014. The decline is a key area for management investigation.
3. Bikes dominate revenue: The product analysis shows Bikes contributing overwhelmingly more revenue than Components,
Clothing and Accessories. Road Bikes and Mountain Bikes are particularly important subcategories.
4. Geographic performance differs: Southwest is the leading visible territory by revenue, followed by Canada and Northwest.
This indicates that sales are not evenly distributed across markets.
5. Volume does not tell the whole story: The product scatter analysis shows differences between quantity sold and revenue
generated. Management should therefore consider both unit volume and sales value when evaluating products.

## Business Recommendations

1. Investigate the 2014 revenue decline by reviewing product demand, territory performance, order volume and changes in
product mix.
2. Protect and grow the core bicycle categories, particularly Road Bikes and Mountain Bikes, through demand monitoring,
inventory planning and targeted promotions.
3. Compare leading and weaker territories to identify differences in product mix and demand, then adapt successful approaches
to weaker markets where appropriate.
4. Use both revenue and quantity when making product decisions. High-volume products should not automatically be treated as
the most valuable products without considering sales value.

## Conclusion
The analysis combines SQL, Pandas and Power BI to provide a focused view of AdventureWorks' sales and product
performance. The main business story is that revenue is strongly driven by bicycle products, territory performance varies
considerably, and the significant decline after the 2013 peak deserves further investigation. The dashboard allows management
to move from overall sales performance into product-level analysis and identify areas requiring attention.
